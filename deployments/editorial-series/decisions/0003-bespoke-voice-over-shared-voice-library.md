# 0003 Bespoke voice over the shared voice library

**Status** Accepted

## Context

The sibling editorial-publishing deployment carries a library of five voices with a per-channel menu the model chooses from. The culture series is a different genre, lived-experience essays anchored on human friction rather than deal news, and early drafts written through the shared library read like reports.

## Options

1. Reuse the shared voice library and add a culture voice to the menu.
2. Give the series its own single locked voice, no menu, permanently divergent from the shared library.

## Decision

Option 2. A series is recognisable by sounding the same every week, so a menu is a liability rather than a feature here. The locked voice opens on the culture misread, carries the lesson through one named person, caps numbers as texture, and hard-bans enforcement and compliance framing in the body. The ban sits at the write prompt, not the shared research stage, so research stays reusable.

## Consequences

Two voice systems now exist and do not share prompt text, an accepted maintenance cost. Series output is consistent week to week. Any voice improvement in one pipeline must be consciously ported to the other or deliberately left out.

## Revisit if

The series expands to formats that need voice variety, or the two voice systems converge in practice and the duplication stops paying.
