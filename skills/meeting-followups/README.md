# Meeting Follow-ups

**Type** Cowork skill  ·  **Trigger** Working the engaged stage of the CRM board, the manual lane the follow-up agent does not touch

## What it does

Runs the follow-up cycle on leads that have already met or are about to, the stage where momentum matters more than new information. Each card gets a complete cycle, read everything, reconstruct the conversation, draft the touch, set the next due date, write the ledger back. Touches are short, warm and closely spaced while a concrete step is in the air, stretching out when the ball is clearly on the lead's side.

## Flow

```
engaged-stage card comes due
   |
read the card and every comment
   |
sweep the mail threads, every contact and the domain
   |
full picture, who spoke last, what is open, whose side
   |
draft the touch in the house register
   |
write back, ledger comment + next due date
   |
next card (one at a time, never bulk)
```

## How it runs

**One card at a time, never in bulk.** The cycle is read the card completely including every comment, sweep the email threads for every contact on the card and the company domain, draw the full picture before touching anything, who spoke last, what question is open and on whose side it sits, then draft, then write the action and the next due date back to the card in the same pass. Each stage of the pipeline has its own follow-up doctrine and none inherits another's, cold-stage rules never gate an engaged-stage momentum nudge.

## Guardrails

A future due date means the card sleeps, it exits the cycle untouched. Comments on the card are binding instructions, a park agreed with the lead is honoured whatever the due date suggests. If any referenced email cannot be found in the connected mailbox the cycle stops and asks, some mail lives in unconnected sending mailboxes and drafting against an incomplete picture is forbidden. A draft must move the conversation forward, never repeat an open question, never regress to an earlier state, never reopen a closed front.

## Lessons carried

Every rule above exists because its absence caused a real incident. A bulk read that skipped comments nearly fired follow-ups at two leads whose cards carried agreed seasonal parks. A draft written from the connected mailbox alone re-asked a question the lead already had on the table, because the actual last email had gone out from an unconnected mailbox. Token efficiency is explicitly not a variable in this procedure, the per-card cycle is slower and that is the point.
